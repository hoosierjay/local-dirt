**Forsyth County Sheriff’s Office – New FY 26 Justice Assistance Grant (JAG) Funding**  
The Sheriff’s Office is rolling out the Edward Byrne Memorial Justice Assistance Grant for FY 26, aimed at bolstering community‑focused public‑safety programs. Residents can review the grant details and submit comments via the online form.
